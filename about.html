About Me

Researcher, Engineer, and Educator dedicated to intelligent, sustainable, and inclusive technologies.

Dr. Rupendra Pratap Singh Hada

Assistant Professor, IILM University Gurugram • PhD, IIT Indore

I am an Assistant Professor in the School of Computer Science and Engineering at IILM University, Gurugram. I hold a PhD in Computer Science from the Indian Institute of Technology Indore (2021–2025).

My doctoral work addressed GPS-free localisation and energy-efficient routing in Wireless Sensor Networks using machine learning and metaheuristic optimisation, with edge computing and IoT security as allied threads. As a Visiting Researcher at the Western Norway Research Institute, I worked on indoor and outdoor localisation and environmental sensing.
